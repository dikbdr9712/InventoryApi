# Terms of Use and Privacy

These terms apply when you create an account and shop with DK/Phar Inventory Management System ("DK/Phar", "we", "us"). By creating an account you accept them.

## 1. Your account
- Give your real name, email and phone number, and keep your password secret. You are responsible for orders placed with your account.
- Tell us at once if you think someone else is using your account.

## 2. Orders and prices
- Prices are in Ngultrum (Nu.) and are shown before you order. Some products are sold by other shops (marketplace sellers); the product page shows who sells it.
- An order with products from several sellers is delivered in several packages. A delivery fee may apply for each package; it is shown before you pay.
- We may cancel an order if a product is out of stock or a price was clearly wrong. If you have paid, we refund you in full.

## 3. Payment and delivery
- Online orders are paid before delivery, by bank transfer. Enter the journal number from your bank receipt so we can check your payment.
- When your package arrives, give the driver the 4-digit delivery code from your order page, and only after you have the package in your hands.

## 4. Returns and refunds
- If a product is wrong, damaged, missing or not as described, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund you after checking.
- Refunds go back to you by bank transfer or in cash at the counter.

## 5. Your information (privacy)
- We collect your name, email, phone number, delivery address and order history to run your account, deliver your orders, take payments and answer your messages.
- We share your name, phone number and delivery address only with the seller and the driver who need them for your order. We do not sell your information.
- Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed); nobody at DK/Phar can read them.
- You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, unless we must keep it by law (for example sales records for tax).

## 6. Fair use
- Do not misuse the website, for example by trying to get into other accounts, sending false payment details or placing orders you do not intend to pay for. We may close accounts that do.

## 7. Changes
- We may update these terms. The version you accepted is kept with your account.

## 8. Law
- These terms follow the laws of the Kingdom of Bhutan.

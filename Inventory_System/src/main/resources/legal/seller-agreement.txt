# Seller Agreement

This agreement is between you (the "Seller") and DP DrukBazaars ("we", "us"), which runs the online shop where you list and sell your products. By ticking "I agree" and sending your application you accept this agreement.

## 1. Who can sell
- You are at least 18 years old and allowed to run a business in Bhutan.
- The details you give us (name, CID number, trade licence, TPN, pickup address, bank account) are true and belong to you or to your registered business.
- We may ask for documents and may refuse or close an account at our discretion, for example if details cannot be checked.

## 2. Your products
- You only list products you own and are allowed to sell. You do not list fake, stolen, expired, unsafe, illegal or restricted goods, or anything that needs a licence you do not hold.
- Names, photos, descriptions, prices and stock must be honest and kept up to date. A price you set is the price the customer pays.
- You are responsible for the quality and safety of your products and for any labels or warnings the law requires.
- We may hide or remove a product that breaks these rules, without notice.

## 3. Orders and packing
- Customers pay DP DrukBazaars before delivery. You will see an order under "To pack" only after the payment has been checked.
- You pack each order carefully, with exactly the items ordered, and mark it "Packed" promptly (within one working day unless agreed otherwise).
- A DP DrukBazaars driver collects the package from your pickup address. Hand packages only to the driver shown in your dashboard.
- If you cannot fulfil an order, tell us at once so we can cancel it and refund the customer.

## 4. Commission and payment
- For every delivered order we keep a commission: a percentage of the selling price of the items (before GST). The current rate is shown on the "Sell with us" page and in your dashboard. We will tell you before a new rate applies; a rate change applies only to new orders.
- Your earning for an order is the selling price minus the commission. It is added to your balance when the customer has received the package.
- We pay your balance by bank transfer to the account you gave us, normally once a week. You can see every earning and payment in your dashboard.
- If a customer's money is refunded because of a fault with your product (wrong, damaged, missing or not as described), the refunded amount is taken from your balance.
- You are responsible for your own taxes on your income, including any GST you must charge and declare.

## 5. Returns and complaints
- You accept returns that follow the DP DrukBazaars returns policy shown to customers, and you help us sort out complaints about your products quickly and politely.

## 6. Customer information
- You use customer names and details only to fulfil their orders. You do not contact customers for other purposes, sell or share their details, or keep them longer than needed.

## 7. Suspension and ending
- We may pause or close your account if you break this agreement, receive repeated complaints, or put customers at risk. We will tell you why.
- You may stop selling at any time by contacting us. Orders already paid must still be packed, and we pay any balance we owe you after open orders are finished.

## 8. Liability
- DP DrukBazaars provides the online shop, payment collection and delivery. We are not the maker of your products and are not responsible for their quality.
- Neither side is responsible for delays caused by events outside its control (for example natural disasters, road closures or network failures).

## 9. Changes to this agreement
- We may update this agreement. We will show you the new version in your dashboard, and you must accept it to keep selling. Each version is kept, so you can always see what you agreed to.

## 10. Law
- This agreement follows the laws of the Kingdom of Bhutan. We will first try to settle any disagreement by talking with you in good faith.

# Delivery Driver Agreement

This agreement is between you (the "Driver") and DK/Phar Inventory Management System ("DK/Phar", "we", "us"). You deliver packages from sellers and from our shop to customers, and choose which jobs you take. By ticking "I agree" and sending your application you accept this agreement.

## 1. Who can deliver
- You are at least 18 years old.
- If you use a motor vehicle you hold a valid driving licence for it, and the vehicle is registered, roadworthy and insured as the law requires. You keep these valid while you deliver and tell us at once if any of them ends.
- The details you give us (name, CID number, licence, vehicle, bank account, emergency contact) are true and belong to you.
- We may ask for documents and may refuse or close an account at our discretion.

## 2. How jobs work
- Open jobs show where to collect, where to deliver and what the job pays. Taking a job is your choice.
- Once you accept a job, collect it promptly. If you cannot do it, give it back in the app before collecting it so another driver can take it.
- Customers pay before delivery. Never ask a customer for money.
- Hand the package over only to the customer (or the person they name), and only after they give you the 4-digit delivery code from their order page. Enter the code to finish the job. Never guess or ask the shop for the code.

## 3. Care of packages
- Keep packages safe, dry and closed. Do not open them.
- If a package is lost or damaged while you carry it because of your carelessness, the cost may be taken from your earnings after we have talked with you.

## 4. Safety and conduct
- Follow all traffic rules. Never use your phone while driving. Do not deliver under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Be polite to customers and sellers. Do not use their phone numbers or addresses for anything except the delivery.
- Your safety comes first: if a delivery is unsafe (weather, road conditions, threats), stop and contact us.

## 5. Pay
- You earn the delivery pay shown on each job when the package is delivered with the correct code.
- We pay your balance by bank transfer to the account you gave us, normally once a week. You can see every earning and payment in your dashboard.
- You are an independent delivery partner, not an employee. You pay your own fuel, phone and vehicle costs, and you are responsible for your own taxes.

## 6. Suspension and ending
- We may pause or close your account if you break this agreement, your licence or vehicle papers are no longer valid, or customers complain repeatedly. We will tell you why.
- You may stop at any time by contacting us, after finishing or giving back the jobs you hold. We pay any balance we owe you.

## 7. Information we keep
- We keep your details, documents and delivery records to run deliveries, pay you and meet the law. Only DK/Phar staff who need them can see your documents.

## 8. Changes to this agreement
- We may update this agreement. We will show you the new version in your dashboard, and you must accept it to keep taking jobs. Each version is kept, so you can always see what you agreed to.

## 9. Law
- This agreement follows the laws of the Kingdom of Bhutan. We will first try to settle any disagreement by talking with you in good faith.
